## About

33 Koti is an exploratory research project investigating whether the 33 traditional categories of Devas (12 Adityas, 8 Vasus, 11 Rudras, 2 Ashvins) can be understood as **recurring inner patterns** — qualities that appear across individuals, families, generations and regions, and that may change, combine or reappear over time.

The project began during the COVID-19 lockdown, through conversations with friends from different states of India, when the same kinds of qualities seemed to appear again and again in different families.

**Core idea:** `Category ≠ Person`. A category is a *possible pattern of qualities*, not a permanent label for a human being.

## What the study looks at

- Recurring and spontaneous thoughts
- Subconscious and emotional tendencies
- Intuition, choices and reactions
- Families → Generation 1 → 2 → 3 → 4
- Comparison across different states of India

```
Generation 1 → A
Generation 2 → A + F
Generation 3 → C + F + H
```

## What this project is NOT claiming

- That the 33 categories are scientifically proven
- That every family belongs to one permanent category
- That qualities are necessarily inherited genetically
- That geography or personal observation alone proves a supernatural mechanism

Other explanations (upbringing, culture, environment, personality, genetics, chance) are part of the investigation. The guiding question is also: **"What evidence would show that I am wrong?"**

These are hypotheses, not established scientific conclusions.

## Deploy on GitHub Pages

1. Create a new repository and upload `index.html` and `README.md`.
2. Go to **Settings → Pages**.
3. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**, select `main` and `/ (root)`, then save.
4. After a minute your site will be live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/`.
